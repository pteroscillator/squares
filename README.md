# Squares

A ball bounces across a grid of squares and each square it crosses plays a short tone. Move the mouse or a finger over the grid to play it yourself.

Live: https://pteroscillator.github.io/squares/

Browsers tested: Safari and Firefox on macOS, Safari on iPhone

Web Audio behaves differently on Safari and iOS. On iOS, the sound plays even with the silent switch on (Safari 16.4 or later).
